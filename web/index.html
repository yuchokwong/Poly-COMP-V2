Why compare them?

Strategy B needs 6,378 human-written captions that a new meme will never have. Strategy C needs GPU hours but generalises to unseen images. Strategy A needs nothing at all. The evaluation reports accuracy next to this knowledge cost, latency, and paired bootstrap significance.
